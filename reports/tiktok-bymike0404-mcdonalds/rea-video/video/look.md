## Camera, lighting and editing

- Visual style: Bold flat motion graphics in McDonald's red and yellow, comic sunbursts and burst shapes, real product photos cut out and composited into UI cards, light 3D for the opener
- Framing: shot size medium close-up 100%; subject is the in-focus region (no face; shot size is an estimate)
- Camera: moving camera (gimbal / handheld pan) 94%, fixed camera (tripod / mount / on the desk) 6%; typical move push-in
- Lighting: night / low-light ambient, no dedicated fill. Key light frontal (from the camera side; both sides of the subject about equally bright), from above (ceiling light / street lamp / high source); lighting ratio about 1.1:1 (0.1 stops); soft light (almost no lit / shadow edge); colour temperature warm (about 3000–4000K); the light in the dark carries strong colour: neon signs or RGB mood lights; background clearly blurred (shallow depth of field / portrait mode)
- (not live footage: the above describes the light in the picture, for generation prompts, not a shooting plan)
- Grade: natural (close to the original colours); high saturation, normal contrast, normal blacks, overall warm
- Editing: 42.0 cuts per minute, 1.34s average shot; no live-action shots
