# Kopi Ratu fonts — free for commercial use

Matched to the "Bloom" lettering sample (source/brand/font-sample-bloom.jpg): an upright, narrow, rounded
1970s serif with flared, ball-ended strokes. Specimen: `kopi-ratu-font-pairing.png`.

| Role | Font | Licence | Download (TTF for Canva, Illustrator, CapCut, Word…) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Display: headlines, big words | **Chicle** | SIL OFL 1.1 | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Chicle |
| 2 · Body: text, quotes, buttons | **Gelasio** (Regular, Italic, Bold) | SIL OFL 1.1 | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Gelasio |
| Optional accent: one word at a time | **Sansita Swashed** | SIL OFL 1.1 | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Sansita+Swashed |

- **SIL Open Font License 1.1** lets you use these fonts free in anything, commercial included: video,
  social posts, packaging, print, web, logos. You may embed them in files and bundle them with products.
  You may not sell the font files on their own. The full licence text is in the `*-OFL.txt` files here.
- **Gelasio** is drawn to Georgia's shapes and spacing, so it keeps the brand's Georgia look while being
  free to share and embed (Georgia itself comes with Windows and Mac but cannot be redistributed).
- **Chicle** is the closest free match to the sample. Unlike the sample, its "B" has no curl.
- **Sansita Swashed** has the curled "B" but is slanted; use it for a single accent word, not a headline.

The `.woff2` files here are the web versions (used by the videos in this repository). For desktop apps,
download the TTF from the links above.
